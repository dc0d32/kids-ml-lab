the fox and the grapes

a hungry fox saw a bunch of grapes hanging high on a vine. he jumped once and missed. he jumped twice and missed. he jumped a third time and missed again. he walked away with his nose in the air and said those grapes were sour anyway. it is easy to hate what you cannot have.


the tortoise and the hare

a hare laughed at a tortoise for being slow. the tortoise said let us race. the hare ran so far ahead that he lay down for a nap. the tortoise kept walking and walking and never stopped. when the hare woke up the tortoise had already crossed the line. slow and steady wins the race.


the lion and the mouse

a lion caught a mouse under his paw. the mouse begged to be let go and promised to help one day. the lion laughed but let the mouse go. later the lion was caught in a hunters net. the mouse came and chewed through the ropes until the lion was free. a small friend can do a big thing.


the boy who cried wolf

a shepherd boy was bored so he shouted wolf wolf. the villagers ran up the hill and found no wolf at all. he did it again and they came again and again there was no wolf. then a real wolf came and the boy shouted with all his voice. nobody came. a liar is not believed even when he tells the truth.


the ant and the grasshopper

all summer the grasshopper sang while the ant carried grain into her nest. when winter came the grasshopper had nothing to eat and knocked on the ants door. the ant said you sang all summer so now you may dance all winter. there is a time to work and a time to play.


the crow and the pitcher

a thirsty crow found a pitcher with a little water at the bottom. her beak could not reach it. she picked up pebbles one by one and dropped them in. the water rose and rose until she could drink. little by little does the trick.


the wind and the sun

the wind and the sun argued about who was stronger. they saw a traveller in a coat and agreed that whoever could remove the coat would win. the wind blew hard and cold and the traveller only pulled the coat tighter. then the sun shone warm and gentle and the traveller took the coat off himself. kindness does what force cannot.


the dog and his reflection

a dog carried a bone across a bridge. he looked down and saw another dog with another bone in the water. he barked to take it and his own bone fell in and sank. he ended up with nothing. greed can lose you what you already have.


the town mouse and the country mouse

a town mouse visited his cousin in the country and laughed at the plain food. come to the city he said and eat well. in the city the food was rich but a cat came twice and a dog came once. the country mouse went home and said i would rather eat beans in peace than cake in fear.


the goose that laid the golden eggs

a farmer had a goose that laid one golden egg each morning. he grew impatient and thought there must be a pile of gold inside. he cut the goose open and found nothing at all and now there were no more eggs. do not spoil a good thing by wanting all of it at once.


the milkmaid and her pail

a milkmaid walked to market with a pail on her head. she thought i will sell this milk and buy eggs and the eggs will become chickens and the chickens will buy me a fine dress. she tossed her head proudly and the pail fell and the milk spilled. do not count your chickens before they hatch.


the fox and the crow

a crow sat in a tree with a piece of cheese in her beak. a fox below said what a beautiful bird you are surely your voice is beautiful too. the crow opened her beak to sing and the cheese fell straight down. the fox ate it and walked away. beware of flattery.


the frogs and the well

two frogs lived in a pond that dried up. one said let us jump into that deep well there is water down there. the other said and how would we get out if that dries up too. think about the way out before you jump in.


the bundle of sticks

an old farmer gave each of his sons a stick and told them to break it. they all snapped easily. then he tied the sticks into a bundle and asked them to break that. not one of them could. together we hold what alone we cannot.


the shepherd and the sea

a shepherd sold his sheep and bought a boat full of dates to sell across the water. a storm came and he threw everything overboard to stay afloat. he came home with nothing and went back to keeping sheep. later a friend praised the calm sea and the shepherd said yes it is calm because it wants more dates.


the two travellers and the bear

two friends walked through a forest and met a bear. one climbed a tree at once. the other fell flat and held his breath and the bear sniffed him and left. the friend in the tree asked what did the bear whisper. he said the bear told me never to travel with a friend who leaves at the first sign of trouble.


the hares and the frogs

the hares were sad because everything frightened them and they thought no creature was more timid. they ran to the lake to hide and all the frogs leapt away in terror at the sound. the hares stopped and said there is always someone more afraid than we are.


the old lion and the fox

an old lion pretended to be sick so animals would visit and he could eat them. a fox stopped at the door and would not come in. the lion asked why. the fox said i see many footprints going in and none coming out.


the peacock and the crane

a peacock spread his shining tail and laughed at the plain grey crane. the crane rose into the air and said your feathers are lovely but i can fly. beauty on the ground is not worth much in the sky.


the boy and the nuts

a boy put his hand into a jar of nuts and grabbed as many as he could. his fist was too full to come out. he cried until someone told him to take half as many. do not try to take everything at once.


the mice in council

the mice met to decide what to do about the cat. one clever mouse said let us hang a bell around the cats neck so we hear him coming. everyone cheered. then an old mouse asked who will hang the bell. a plan is not a plan until someone can do it.


the donkey in the lion skin

a donkey found a lion skin and wore it and frightened everyone in the field. he was so pleased that he brayed and everyone knew him at once. you can wear a costume but your voice is still your own.


the stag at the pool

a stag admired his fine antlers in a pool and complained about his thin legs. a hunter came and his legs carried him fast until his antlers caught in a branch. what we prize is not always what saves us.


the farmer and the stork

a farmer set a net for the cranes eating his seed and caught a stork among them. the stork begged saying i am not a crane i am a good bird. the farmer said perhaps but i caught you with them. you are judged by the company you keep.


the fox and the goat

a fox fell into a well and could not get out. a goat came by and asked if the water was good. excellent said the fox come down. the goat jumped in and the fox climbed out on his back. look before you leap and be careful whose advice you take.


the eagle and the arrow

an eagle was struck by an arrow and saw that the arrow was fitted with eagle feathers. he said how sad that we give our enemies the means to harm us.


the horse and the stag

a horse and a stag quarrelled over a meadow. the horse asked a man for help and let the man put a saddle on his back so they could chase the stag away. they won the meadow but the horse never got the saddle off again. do not trade your freedom for a small victory.


the miser and his gold

a miser buried his gold at the foot of a tree and dug it up each day just to look at it. one day it was gone and he wept. a neighbour said bury a stone in its place and look at that instead since you never used the gold anyway.


the oak and the reeds

a great oak stood proud beside a bank of thin reeds. a storm came and the reeds bent low and let the wind pass over. the oak stood stiff and was torn out by the roots. it is better to bend than to break.


the cat and the birds

a cat heard that the birds in an aviary were sick so he dressed as a doctor and knocked with his bag of tools. the birds looked out and said we shall be much better if you go away.


the lion and the gnat

a tiny gnat challenged a lion and stung him on the nose until the lion tore at his own face with his claws. the gnat flew off singing about his victory and flew straight into a spiders web. do not celebrate so hard that you forget to watch where you are going.


the dove and the ant

an ant fell into a stream and a dove dropped a leaf for her to climb on. later a hunter aimed at the dove and the ant bit his foot so he missed. one good turn deserves another.
